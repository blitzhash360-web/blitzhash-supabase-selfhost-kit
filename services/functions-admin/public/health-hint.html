Host & container health

The kit starts Glances (host CPU/RAM/disk + containers) and Dozzle (live container logs) by default. Both sit behind their own auth and bind to localhost unless you change the overlay.

  • Glances: http://127.0.0.1:61208
  • Dozzle: http://127.0.0.1:8080

Credentials match your Studio dashboard user/password unless you override them. See docs/HEALTH.md.
